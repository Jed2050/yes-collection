The Maison

A house built
on precision.

YES Collection is not a label — it is a discipline. Every piece begins with a single measurement and ends with a hand-stitched signature. We exist to give shape to ambition.

Our Origin

From a single
fitting room.

The house was founded on a refusal — a refusal to accept that a man should compromise between fit, soul, and time. We started with one tailor, one client, one piece. The discipline has not changed.

Today, our atelier serves a small, intentional roster of clients across the Caribbean and beyond — heads of state, performers, grooms, and the quietly extraordinary. Each garment is treated as the only one we will make this week.

Our Code

Three
commitments.

Uncompromised Material

We source exclusively from the mills of Biella, Yorkshire and Como. Italian Super 130s wool, English worsted, mulberry silk, mother-of-pearl. No substitutions, ever.

Hand Over Machine

Forty-two hours of hand-stitching per piece. Half-canvas chest construction. Working surgeon cuffs. Pick-stitched lapels. The shortcuts that make modern suits cheap are the shortcuts we refuse.

For a Lifetime

Every YES garment includes lifetime alterations. As your body changes, the suit follows. As fashion turns, the cut endures. We are not in the business of disposable elegance.

The Process

From measure
to moment.

Four stages. Twelve weeks. One garment that will outlive the moment it was made for.

N° 01

Consult

A private 90-minute session with a master tailor. Fabric, silhouette, intention.

N° 02

Measure

Twenty-eight individual measurements taken by hand. No estimations, no shortcuts.

N° 03

Construct

Forty-two hours of hand-stitching. Half-canvas chest piece. Cut, basted, refined.

N° 04

Deliver

Final fitting, signed certificate of authenticity, lifetime alterations included.
